        Craps Trainer

        Practise craps with your own dice. Throw real dice at your table, put in what they show (or let a camera read them), and the app plays the table: it settles your bets in play money, the stickman calls the roll, and every throw is logged so you can see whether your dice sets and positions do better than fair dice.

        Putting in a throw

        • Tap the two dice on the dice card, left die first, then Roll. On a keyboard, type them (6 2) and press Enter.
        • Put in the wrong dice? Tap the right ones and press Fix last (F2). Undo (Ctrl+Z) takes the last throw back.
        • Virtual roll throws random dice for you, to play the bets. Virtual rolls don't count in your stats.

        The table

        • Pick a chip from the tray under the layout, then tap a spot to bet it. To take a bet down or move it, press and hold it (on a computer: right-click it, or drag it).
        • The bankroll tile sets your buy-in. Rebuy adds another, Reset bankroll starts over, and Run stats shows how the session went, bet by bet.

        The tracker

        • Before you throw, choose your Dice set and how you hold the dice ("face up - face toward you": 6-2 is sixes on top with the twos facing you), your Position at the table, and whether the dice hit the back wall. Each throw is logged with them, from either tab.
        • The Tracker tab shows the shooter's run, the Small / Tall / All bonus, your totals against fair dice, and, under Compared by, your sevens and numbers for each dice set or position, with whether they're better than random.

        The camera

        Start camera shows the table through this device's camera; Picture shows or hides it. On accounts set up for it, it also reads the dice, so throws go in by themselves (Fix last puts a wrong read right). Otherwise, tap the dice in.

        Players, voice and sound

        Players… in the name list adds guests, who play on your screen with their own bankroll and stats. Stickman picks who calls the rolls, and Sound sets the volumes.

        From the menu

        Download your throws as a CSV file, see what we keep about you, and send a feature request or a bug report. This page is in the menu too, under How it works.

        Players at your table

        Guests play on your screen with their own bankroll and stats. Send a guest their history and it moves to an account of their own.
