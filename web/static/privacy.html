What we keep about you

Craps Trainer is a hobby project for practising craps with your own dice. It's play money only: there's no real gambling and nothing to buy.

What's stored

  • Your email address, so we can send you sign-in links. We don't send anything else and never share it.
  • The name you pick, and the names of any guests you add.
  • Your throws and your table: the dice, when you threw them, the dice set and position you chose, your play bankroll and bets, and your settings.
  • Dice pictures, only when a camera reads your throws, to help the camera read dice better.
  • Sign-in records: when a link was asked for, from which internet address (to stop anyone flooding an inbox with sign-in emails), and which devices are signed in. Old link records are deleted after a week.

A small cookie keeps each device signed in. There are no ads and no trackers. If the sign-in page shows a bot check, it is run by Cloudflare Turnstile.

Your choices

  • Download every throw as a CSV file from the menu, whenever you like.
  • Delete your account from the menu. It removes your email, your guests and all your throws for good (backups that already hold them are replaced within a few weeks).
